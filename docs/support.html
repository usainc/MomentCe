Contact

For help with MomentCe, email usaincelik@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue.

Common Questions

  • Notifications: Check that notifications are enabled for MomentCe in iOS Settings.
  • Event data: Event and reminder data is stored locally by default. Optional iCloud backup depends on your iCloud settings.
  • Ads and consent: Ad consent choices may be shown through Google User Messaging Platform where required.
  • Deleting data: You can remove events inside the app. Deleting the app may remove local app data from the device.
