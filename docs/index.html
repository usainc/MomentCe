MomentCe is an iOS app for tracking birthdays, anniversaries, countdowns, and meaningful dates. This page collects the public support and policy links used for App Store review and user support.

What MomentCe Does

  • Tracks birthdays, anniversaries, countdowns, and special dates.
  • Helps users plan reminders and notifications for meaningful days.
  • Stores user-created event data locally by default, with optional iCloud backup if enabled.
  • May show ads through Google AdMob, as described in the Privacy Policy.
